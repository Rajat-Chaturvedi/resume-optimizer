Senior Backend Engineer, Payments Platform

We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to build and scale distributed systems that power our global payments platform. You will design microservices, own reliability, and partner with product and data teams.

Responsibilities
- Design and build distributed systems and REST APIs in Go or Java
- Own service reliability, observability, and incident response using Datadog and Prometheus
- Build data pipelines with Kafka and Spark to support real-time fraud detection
- Deploy and operate services on Kubernetes with Terraform-managed infrastructure as code
- Drive system design reviews and mentor engineers across teams
- Partner cross functionally with product managers and designers

Qualifications
- 6+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong experience with Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, and AWS
- Expertise in Go, Java, or Python and a solid grasp of data structures and system design
- Experience with PostgreSQL, Redis, and event driven architectures
- Experience with continuous integration and continuous delivery pipelines
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent experience
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
